#CRM Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing CRM data to understand lead
conversion, lead sources, opportunity performance, sales pipeline,
win/loss trends, and expected revenue.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed with two main
sections:

- Lead Analytics
- Opportunity Performance

## Business Objectives

- Analyze total leads and converted leads
- Measure lead conversion rate
- Identify high-performing lead sources
- Analyze leads across industries
- Understand lead status distribution
- Track total opportunities
- Analyze win and loss rates
- Analyze opportunities by pipeline stage
- Understand lost opportunity reasons
- Analyze expected revenue by stage
- Compare won and lost opportunities

## Key KPIs

### Lead Analytics

- Total Leads
- Converted Leads
- Converted Accounts
- Average Lead Score
- Converted Opportunities
- Conversion Rate

### Opportunity Performance

- Total Opportunities
- Active Opportunities
- Win Rate
- Loss Rate
- Total Won Revenue
- Average Days to Close

## Dashboard Features

### Lead Analytics

- Lead conversion analysis
- Lead source analysis
- Lead status analysis
- Industry-wise lead analysis
- Conversion rate by lead source

### Opportunity Performance

- Opportunity pipeline funnel
- Won vs Lost opportunity analysis
- Expected revenue by stage

- Industry filter

The dashboard highlights several important CRM metrics.

- Total leads analyzed: 10,000

- Total opportunities: 4,646
- Active opportunities: 1,272
- Win rate: 42.8%
- Loss rate: 57.23%
- Total won revenue: 136M
- Average days to close: 100
